This video shows you the features of the History Log of a certain PID. You can access any individual log and email the details.
These logs cannot be modified once they are submitted. You can also create a new log, where you can record comments and add pictures by taking a picture through the app. You can also edit your local date and time, which is converted to CST for storage purposes.
Upon saving, an email is automatically generated with the details of the picture you added.
